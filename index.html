⚠️ Version honesty (Mod Kit vs UE5)

FeatureMod Kit (UE 4.20.3) — use thisModern UE5 tutorials — do NOT copy blindly
SkyDirectional + Sky Light + Sky Atmosphere + Exponential Height Fog. Early kits had Atmospheric Fog instead — same idea.Lumen, Volumetric Clouds. You don't have them.
LightingStatic / Stationary / Movable + mandatory Build Lighting + Lightmass Volume.Lumen often skips bake. You must bake.
InputProject Settings → Input → Action Mappings (E).Enhanced Input. Not in your kit.
Neighbor AIBP_AreaNeighborHouse / BP_AreaPlayerHouse / BP_GeneralSwitch auto-detected.Build from scratch. You are lucky.
ModelsFBX 2014–2018, TGA/PNG, no Nanite.Nanite, glTF. Stick to FBX.

❓ FAQ

Where are the Neighbor house props? I don't want to model yet

Content Browser → search SM_Wall, BP_Door, BP_Key, BP_NeighborCharacter, BP_Area* inside the kit content. Drag to level. Model from scratch only in lesson 7.

Everything pink / black. What now?

Pink = missing material (reassign, see troubleshooting). Black after Build = no Lightmass Volume or Static without bake. Add volume, rebuild on Preview.

How do shared community posts work?

Locally: posts live in your browser (localStorage). Hosted + cloud keys in config.js: posts live in Firebase/Supabase and everyone sees the same list. See hosting guide.
