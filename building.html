2. Objects & building your house

Placing objects

Content Browser → search SM_Wall, SM_Floor, SM_Stair, wall, floor in kit folders. Filter by Static Mesh. Drag into Viewport (double-click opens mesh editor — wrong). Details → Transform: Location/Rotation/Scale — use multiples of 100 for walls (400-long wall → next at +400 X). Viewport: W move, E rotate, R scale (RMB released!). Copy: Ctrl+C/V, Alt+drag for rows, Ctrl+G group.

💡
Grid snap

Magnet icon: 50–100 cm walls, 10 cm furniture, 90° rotation. Without snap you get 397.3 cm walls with 3 mm light leaks.

Collision types: Simple (box — fast, walls), Complex (per-triangle — slow, statues), Custom UCX_ from Blender. No Simple = walk-through: open mesh → Collision → Add Box Simplified → Save.

12-step house (800×800, floor 300 high, walls 20 thick, grid 100)

1) Floor 800×800×20 at (0,0,0). 2) 4 walls 800×300×20: N (0,400,150), S (0,-400,150), W/E rotated 90°. 3) Corners: shorten sides to 760 or add 20×20×300 posts, check Top view. 4) Windows: use SM_WallWindow from kit or compose (sill 0–100 + lintel 220–300 + sides, hole 150×120). Never scale holes — breaks collision. 5) Door hole 120×220 in south wall (2 pieces + lintel); opening door comes in puzzles. 6) Slab 800×800×20 at Z=310 (ceiling+2nd floor). 7) Stairs: kit SM_Stairs or 10 steps 100×25×18 shifted 25 cm, slope ~35°, rails as thin boxes, width 120. 8) 2nd floor walls Z=460, leave 120×250 stair hole (2 slabs with gap, not solid!). 9) Roof: 2 slabs 500×500×15 at 30° + gables, ridge ~750, 50 cm overhang. 10) Lights: Point per room 1000/r600 Stationary + Spot over entrance, then Build. 11) Furniture: bed/table/wardrobe, snap 10, wardrobe hides next key. 12) Collision run: walk the perimeter, climb stairs, try windows — no pass-through.

Pivot, Snap, gaps

Pivot should sit at wall corner/base; center pivots misalign snapping. Gaps: light stripes = 1–5 mm miss. Fix with snap + Top view + 1–2 cm overlap (802 wall hides the seam). Fat collision: you stop 20 cm early? Its box is bigger than mesh — recreate Simplified. Scale 1.13: eye-stretched walls never join — reset to 1.0, use correct-length meshes.

Light through joints / falling through 2nd floor / stuck on stairs?

Joints: snap 10 + overlap 2 cm + rebuild light. Floor: check each slab BlockAll. Stairs: step height ≤25 cm + side collision walls.
