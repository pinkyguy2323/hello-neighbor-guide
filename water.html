4. Water, shark & drain puzzle

Pool 10×6×2 m, translucent Panner water, underwater Post, wading-slow player, Trigger shark with Clamp, 8-sec valve drain + WaterDrained. Honest: no built-in swimming in 4.20.3 — we fake it well.

MediumFan favoriteValve finale

📑 Contents

  1. Pool & water material
  2. Player in water
  3. Shark AI (simple, works)
  4. Drain puzzle

Pool & water material

Bowl: floor 1000×600×20 + 4 walls 200 high, water at 150. Tile material Roughness .3, BlockAll, steps + treasure keycard at bottom. Surface: Plane 1000×600 at water Z, NoCollision (or Jesus-walk bug!), Cast Shadow false, Movable (for drain). Material M_MyWater: Translucent, Opacity .5–.7, BaseColor light blue, Roughness .1, Metallic .1, Normal wave via Panner speed .05 (+ second Panner opposite for ripple). Underwater: Post Process Volume sized to bowl (Unbound=false): blue tint, Saturation 1.2, Exposure .9. Second global Post (Unbound=true) = normal grade. Never swap them.

Player in water: 3 options

A — wading (use this): Trigger Volume in pool → Max Walk Speed 400→150 on enter, restore on exit. Cheap, works. B — fake swim: Gravity Scale .3 + Fly while inside (4.20 Character can Swim but no anims — standing pose). C — honest no: full GTA swimming with anims doesn't exist in Mod Kit. Say “wading with slow” — truth. Bonus fear: Apply Damage 5 every 2s inside until drained — player rushes to valve.

Shark AI (simple, works)

BP_Shark: mesh (or cone placeholder first!) + Agro Sphere 1200 + Attack Box 150. Vars: Chasing?, SharkActive=true, Home, Speed 300. Patrol: Timeline 6s Loop Lerp A(−400)→B(400) + yaw. Agro: BeginOverlap → Cast Player → in-water && Active → Chasing=true + Jaws sound; End → false. Skip AI Perception — flaky in kit. Chase on Tick: Chasing && Active && !WaterDrained → Dir = Player−Shark (Z=0) → Normalize × Speed × DeltaTime → SetActorLocation + LookAt yaw. Clamp X/Y to pool every Tick or lag teleports the shark onto the lawn. Z = depth const (bottom+80). Attack: small Box → Apply Damage 50 + Launch + bite sound + 2s cooldown. Player exits (EndOverlap or Z above water): Chasing=false → Timeline home → patrol.

⚠️
Bounds!

Clamp e.g. X ±450, Y ±250 for your pool. Without DeltaTime speed depends on FPS.

Drain puzzle: valve → 8s → safe

BP_Valve: torus mesh + trigger, WaterLevel 1→0, WaterDrained?. E: DoOnce → Play TL_Drain 8s + squeak/water sound + handle 0→720°. Update: WaterLevel=Lerp → Plane Z = bottom + Level×150 + Opacity fade → <.05 = Drained. Also lower/disable the water Post at end. On Drained (Dispatcher or direct ref): SharkActive=false, Chasing=false → thrash anim (Roll ±20° 2s) → Stop → passage lid BlockAll→NoCollision → reveal key + Print “Drained! Safe.” Second E does nothing (DoOnce). WaterDrained is also read by damage tick, shark Tick, Widget hint.

Walk on water / shark flies / no drain / still bites?

Plane BlockAll → NoCollision. Shark: lock Z + Clamp + DeltaTime. No drain: Plane must be Movable + Update wired. Bites after: gate Tick with NOT Drained + set SharkActive=false.
